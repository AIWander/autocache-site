Privacy boundary

Your memory is not our cloud.

Autonomous runs locally. Your Volumes stay where you put them. This page separates what the AutoCache website may receive from what your local product processes.

Effective July 23, 2026 · Private-preview policy
On this page Website data Local product data AI providers Email and support Payments and changes Your choices

The website today

AutoCache.ai currently has no account system, payment checkout, newsletter form, advertising tracker, or first-party analytics. The site does not ask you to upload Volumes, prompts, logs, receipts, or diagnostic archives.

No form submission occurs on this website. Preview and support links open your own email application so you can see and edit exactly what will be sent.

Cloudflare hosts and protects the site. Like most web infrastructure, Cloudflare may process standard request information such as IP address, browser details, requested URL, timing, and security signals under its own policies. External links may expose normal referral and request information to their destination.

What Autonomous processes locally

The Autonomous server, its configuration, your Volumes, local indexes, logs, and receipts run on infrastructure you control. AutoCache does not automatically receive those materials merely because you installed or used the product.

  • Starter Volumes are synthetic and separate from your personal knowledge.
  • You choose the Volumes root and which supported clients can connect.
  • Maintenance tasks are separately previewed and activated; they do not silently upload your corpus.
  • Backups remain your responsibility unless a future service expressly says otherwise.

Your AI provider is a separate boundary

When you connect Autonomous to a cloud AI client, the client may send prompts and selected retrieved context to that AI provider. That processing is governed by your account, settings, contract, and the provider's terms—not by this website privacy statement.

Review each connected client's data controls before exposing sensitive knowledge. Local-first storage does not make a cloud model local.

Email and support

If you email us, AIWander receives your address, message, and anything you attach. We use it to respond, evaluate preview access, provide support, protect the product, and maintain the resulting business record.

Do not send credentials, full Volumes, private prompts, raw transcripts, or unredacted diagnostic archives. We may ask for a narrow, redacted reproduction instead.

Access is limited to AIWander collaborators and service providers who need the message to operate email, secure records, or resolve your request. We keep it only while needed for the request, product security, reasonable follow-up, and applicable legal or accounting obligations, then delete or de-identify it when those purposes end.

Payments, downloads, and policy changes

Payments are not active today. Before checkout launches, this policy will identify the payment and delivery providers involved and the data needed to complete a purchase. AutoCache does not intend to store full payment-card details itself.

Download hosting may produce ordinary access and security logs. Material policy changes will be dated on this page before or when the related feature becomes active.

Your choices

You can browse without contacting us, choose what to write in an email, keep personal data out of support material, disconnect an AI client, change its permissions, or stop using the local product. You may also ask to access, correct, or delete the personal information in your preview or support correspondence, subject to identity verification and records we must retain by law.
